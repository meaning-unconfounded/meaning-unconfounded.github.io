The Instrument

What is live on-chain

EROS is a renounced, fixed-supply token on PulseChain. Nobody can pause it, upgrade it, or pull liquidity. The contracts are public.

Born from the TitanX airdrop on Ethereum. Bridged and bonded on PulseChain. The charts and the burn address are the evidence. The books are the world built around the instrument.

The Instrument · Tokenomics and architecture

Every item below is checkable on-chain today. The full field manual is in The Bridge.

EROS is a fixed-supply token on PulseChain, renounced at launch, with zero founder extraction. Every transfer carries a 5% flow fee, split 2/1/1/1 across burn, staking, a temporary lock, and the core WPLS/EROS liquidity pair.

It was airdropped free to over 10,000 TitanX holders in 2024. If you held TitanX, you may already own EROS. Check the wallet. Two years live. 133 bonded pairs. A liquidity web, not one throne pool.

The bridge carries TitanX-family coins from Ethereum to PulseChain. The bonding pairs every community connection with a doorway. The staking at erosrevival.win rewards the people who stay.

Read the full fee split

2% burn. Removed from supply permanently on every transfer.

1% staking. Rewards distributed to locked EROS via erosrevival.win.

1% temporary lock. Held back briefly, then released to the staking pool.

1% WPLS/EROS LP. Deepens the core liquidity pair so every transaction has a home.

Verify the fee logic on-chain. Verify owner() reads as the zero address. Do not trust a badge.

Why a liquidity web, not one pool

One giant pool means one community, one price, one point of failure. A web of pairs across many TitanX-family coins means each community owns its own doorway. When one pair is quiet, another is active. The web pulls liquidity from wherever it is, into wherever it is needed.
