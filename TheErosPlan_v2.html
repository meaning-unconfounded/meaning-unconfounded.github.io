The physics generates the theology; the theology generates the factions; the factions generate the stories. Each contribution adds another expression of the same underlying architecture.

George Lucas built a universe whose rules could sustain endless stories. Its power came from coherence: the world held together, its principles generated consequences, and every new character or conflict made the larger architecture richer. That is our standard: integrity of world, depth of principle and room for discovery.

Enter the model through whichever door draws you in. Study its principles, challenge its structure, create within its laws and contribute what you discover. The world grows through the work of those who build it.

Section 4 · Fiction

The Rules of the World

Six bullets. Every axiom, formula, and heuristic that governs the Eros cosmos, in compact form.

  1. Existence cannot not-exist. The enclosure is immortal. Nothing truly dies. Everything transforms.
  2. Three infinite spatial dimensions. Depth is the true axis. Outward is motion along the rind, not escape.
  3. All space is filled with subjective quality. Every region has color, temperature, resonance. Nothing is empty.
  4. Consciousness is present at every scale. Awareness + Memory + Choice. Even objects sense, remember, and choose.
  5. Time is the count of iterations. Not a container. Not a tunnel. The count of completed cycles in a region.
  6. Gravity is expansion meeting resistance. Not attraction. The bond that keeps the next state related to the last.

The directional law runs underneath all six: Inwards is Forwards. Every genuine advance (in language, physics, psychology, cosmology) moves inward, into structure.

World-builder's note. These six are the entire physics bible in compressed form. Every scene, every character, every mechanic you build derives from one or more of these. If a story beat violates them, the world breaks. If it strains against them, the world grows. The Field Manual carries the full derivations, formulas, and heuristics.

Section 9

How to Submit

Four rules. One template. One destination.

The Contribution Contract.

Rule 1. Format. Every submission uses the same template: Title / Category Tag / 200-word description / Attachment (image, audio, text, video link).

Rule 2. Tag. Every submission is tagged: Axiom / Character / Visual / Math. No tag, no submission.

Rule 3. Verify. Every submission is tested against the Physics Card (Appendix U of the Field Manual). Pass = listed. Fail = archived with rationale.

Rule 4. Tier. Every submission lands in one of three tiers: Tier 1 Canon / Tier 2 Proposed / Tier 3 Arena.

Where to submit: t.me/Eros_Revival, post in the main channel with the required tag.
