Level 1 · The idea

Three moves. That is the whole thing.

An ERC-20 token crosses the bridge, meets EROS in a PulseX pool, and the pair becomes an LP token you hold. Repeat, then pair the LP tokens, and a tree grows.

1·

Bridge 🌉

Bring the tokens to PulseChain over the official bridge, already built and in daily use. Verify contract addresses first.

2·

Bond 💕

Pair each token with EROS, equal value both sides. You receive, and keep, the LP token: an ordinary ERC-20 receipt.

3·

Branch 🌴

Pair LP tokens with each other, level by level, up to one Final Root LP. Paste an LP address into PulseX’s token search and pair it again.

Level 2 · The thesis

The mathematics builds the tracks. Human psychology is the train.

The community’s market thesis: a narrative about behaviour, not a prediction.

🛡️

Ownership beats liquidity

Bridged tokens start thin. That is a lack of liquidity, not a lack of ownership. The community owns the coins and the LP tokens. Nobody can take them. Establish ownership during the quiet.

🎟️

Slippage is a tollbooth

A buy into a thin pool moves the price by its size. The buyer pays that impact; LPs earn the swap fee. Green candles attract attention , that is the whole attention engine.

🐻

Bear builds, bull thickens

Thin liquidity at the bottom is simply what a bear market looks like: quiet, cheap, the time to build. When volume returns, the pools thicken from the bottom up.

🪜

Upgrade, don’t sell

Swap token A for token B and your position climbs the tree. Each upgrade moves demand one rung up the stack , so climb in stages, never dump a rung.

🔥

Stored value & the cascade

TITANX burns value; DRAGONX is buy-and-burn; HYDRA leverages both confirmed. The thesis: when the public buys the base and upgrades, demand cascades to the thinnest tip.

🤝

Pump, don’t dump

When someone creates a pool, the group follows and adds. The agreed exit is ~1% of a position a day, so the community is never the sell pressure. A convention , not a guarantee.

Level 3 · Architecture

Ten coins. Nineteen pools. One root.

N tokens make N base pools plus N−1 merges , 2N−1 pools. Every merge takes two LP tokens and issues one new one. One wallet can own every layer; nothing is given away at any step.

Unwinding · top-down

Remove the root and you receive its two LP tokens; repeat down the tree, one layer at a time. Each removal returns the pool’s current mix of its two tokens , which may differ from what you put in (impermanent loss). The anchor LP rides straight from Level 2 to the root.

Level 4 · The action plan

From zero to root.

Ten short lessons, in order. The interactive Build Tracker is on the main page.

01 · A pool, and the receipt it gives you

A PulseX V2 pool holds two assets in equal value. Supply both and you receive an LP token, an ordinary token that sits in your wallet and can be paired again. That single fact is why a tree is possible.

02 · The pieces: Lego, not code

EROS, the ecosystem coins, the official bridge and PulseX V2 already exist and already run. The tree adds no contract; only the arrangement is new.

03 · Verify before you move anything

Fake tokens copy real names, and a wrong address spoils every pool built on it. Take each PulseChain address from two official sources, confirm it on PulseScan, and note any transfer tax (use S88, not SCALE).

04 · Bridge in stages

Hold PLS for gas, send one small test, confirm it lands, then bridge the rest in stages.

05 · Get EROS

EROS is the base of every pool, so you need enough to match every coin you pair. Buy in stages and measure from the balance actually in your wallet.

06 · The 4-coin test

Pair EROS with TITANX, DRAGONX, HYDRA and S88 at about $10 a side, nest two LP tokens, then unwind one nested pool and one base pool. Do not scale until all three work.

07 · Level 1: the ten base pools

Pair EROS with each coin at equal USD value per side, and record every LP address, amount and value.

08 · Levels 2 to Root: nest

Nine merges make 19 pools. Level 2: HYDRA+SHOGUN, S88+JAKEX, LEGENDX+BDX, ORX+PULSAR, plus the TITANX+DRAGONX anchor. Then merge upward to one Final Root LP, checking equal value before every supply.

09 · Upgrade, don’t sell

ETH → TITANX → DRAGONX → HYDRA → SCALE/S88. Swapping up moves demand one rung up the stack, and the thin pools at the top move the most. Climb in stages, never all at once.

10 · Hold, follow, exit

Pump, don’t dump: when someone creates a pool, the group follows and adds. Exit slowly: unwind top-down, then ease out about 1% a day. Write the plan down first.
