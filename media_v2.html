Layer A · The serial

The night episodes · a serial, in pairs.

There is also a serial, and it keeps strange hours. Each episode arrives in two lengths, a short that lands the beat, and a long that plays the whole scene.

It opens at 3:47 in the morning, when a code appears in an anomaly and a burn address enters the file. It darkens for eleven minutes while a machine audits a coin it should not care about. It takes a call at 2 AM, when the stick breaks and the cascade begins. And near the end there is a chair in a room that no official is allowed to sit in.

Read one episode the way you would read a dossier found on a desk. The short one first. If the short one keeps you up, the long one is waiting in the room.
