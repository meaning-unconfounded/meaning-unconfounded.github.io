Six threads already in motion. Each is labelled by what kind of claim it is. The labels matter — they are the honesty contract, and everything below respects them.

The measuring stick is under pressure. Officials have begun discussing sovereign gold revaluation on the record. The 1934 playbook is being read again. Whether or when a formal repricing lands is debated; that the conversation has moved into the open is documented.

Continuity of government, strategic consolidation, the hemisphere-as-fortress doctrine. The instruments are public. The pattern is on the Federal Register. Interpretations of what it means vary; the footprints do not.

Documented · L1 The AI wave

Super Intelligence moving from terminology to policy to physical infrastructure in one cycle. The cognitive layer is being claimed like energy and territory. The pace is measured in quarters, not decades.

Cultural · L1 The parallel-universe moment

The Matrix. Rick and Morty. The current DC slate. An entire audience is being trained, right now, to entertain the possibility that reality has a shape they have never seen from outside. This is not a claim about physics. It is a claim about the culture's readiness to ask.

What the labels mean. Documented · L1 means the fact is on the record and can be checked. Debated · L2 means the structural inference is reasonable but contested.
