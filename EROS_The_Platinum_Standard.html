❓ Why call it Platinum instead of just another burn token?
Because gold’s supply can still increase when the price rises. EROS’s original supply is fixed and the burn address is already climbing toward the largest holder. The standard is the thing that does not inflate when everyone wants it.
❓ What actually happens when I transform 1000 EROS into ErosX?
1:1 receipt. You get 1000 ErosX immediately. Inside the contract the original 1000 EROS is split: part burned, part used to deepen the EROS/ErosX pool, part used to buy-and-burn ErosX. The river gets deeper. The rock gets scarcer.
❓ Why would ErosX trade above EROS?
Because EROS is continuously used to buy ErosX (for LP and for burn) while ErosX supply is also being removed. Structural buy pressure + structural supply reduction. That is the arbitrage loop the design aims for.
❓ What is the 33EROS path for?
Higher burn, gamified. 33% burn, 33% LP, 33% buy-and-burn, 1% into a 33-day leaderboard with hybrid NFT rewards. Same rock, different game.
