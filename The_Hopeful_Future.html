The Chain Itself

PulseChain · the substrate

Faster · Cheaper · Stronger · A community-run chain

The Hopeful Future sits on one chain. Not because the other chains are worse. Because this is where the three systems live, and it is where the three systems have decided to keep building.

Why the substrate matters

PulseChain is a proof-of-stake fork of Ethereum, created in 2023 by Richard Heart and the PulseChain community, with the design goal of being faster, cheaper, and stronger than the chain it forked from. Its native token is PLS. Its DEX is PulseX (PLSX). It runs cheap enough that serious projects — including Atropa, EROS, and the entire community-pair ecosystem — can iterate without burning working capital on gas.

Two facts about the chain make it a substrate rather than just a venue:

First, the copy. PulseChain forked Ethereum's state. Every ERC-20 token that existed at the snapshot got a PRC-20 mirror at the same address. This is why the 9iron thesis can name pDAI, FEI, Aave, Maker, and Synthetix contracts at their *original Ethereum addresses* — because PulseChain inherited them. The chain is not just a new chain; it is a copy of an economic state, running on cheaper rails.

Second, the community-run posture. Much of the PulseChain ecosystem runs on community infrastructure — including IPFS-hosted interfaces, community-hosted nodes, and public RPC endpoints. The chain is not dependent on a single corporate operator to stay live. That matters for the same reason the EROS base matters: what holds when the stimulus stops is the same as what holds when the corporate operator stops.

Stated position on the chain's legal posture

The community's stated position, presented here as the community's position and not as verified fact, is as follows. The PulseChain founder, Richard Heart, has contested the jurisdiction of the regulator that sued him and, per the community, has won that jurisdictional fight — the case is being handled in a venue the community considers favorable to PulseChain's actual status. The sacrifice funds contributed during the 2021 launch window — reported by the community as over $2 billion — are held on-chain and, per the community, have not been moved in a way that suggests distress. The community further reports that the chain's tooling is increasingly decentralized (IPFS-hosted interfaces, community nodes, community-run RPCs) so that the chain continues to operate independent of any single operator's legal posture.

This book does not verify these claims. It records the community's stated position and leaves the reader to check the docket, the chain, and the addresses. The reader is asked to apply the same 9iron standard to PulseChain's legal and financial posture as to any other claim in this book: state the claim, mark the layer, state the falsifier, state the confidence. The community's stated confidence in the chain's long-term posture is high. The 9iron standard's response is: verify. On-chain. In the docket. In the block explorer. Do not accept either the FUD or the hopium without looking.

What this book does stand behind. PulseChain is the substrate. The three systems live on it. The three systems are doing serious, dated, checkable work on it. The chain runs cheap enough to iterate on. The chain's community has chosen to build on it despite open legal weather. That is enough to be a substrate. It is not enough to be a settled fact about the future.

The chain is not the point

A chain is not a story. A chain is a floor. The floor is worth noting, but the floor is not the world. PulseChain is the floor. The world is what the three systems build on it.

The base · Atropa

The system that did the work most crypto does not do

The Atropa system counted. It counted 3,141 minter children across five factory generations. It dated them — 2023-05-10 to 2026-09-19. It named their eras — old-atropa, pre-v1, v1, v2, v3-bureau, v4, post-crisis, long-tail. It flagged the flagship datum — the pDAI×Atropa pool burned 93.76% of its LP during the April 2025 crisis window. That last sentence is why Atropa is the base. A system that admits what it cannot measure is a system you can build on.

The base is not a token. It is not a founder. It is not a promise. The base is a graph of verified primitives — burnt LP, dead-address placements, dated events, classified contracts, lineage trees.

The Atropa contribution. Not ownership of the base. The demonstration that the base can be built at all — on a chain where most projects do not bother.

Movement IV

The bond — values

The test of every claim.

The bond · Trust · Frankness · Honesty · Integrity · Zero extraction

One working test, applied everywhere

All of it — base, universe, education — rests on one working test: does it teach and invite, or does it drain and dominate?

The test applies everywhere. On the token, on the story, on the macro rail, on the Archive, on the LP. The bond that holds all of it is not loyalty. It is trust earned by the test passing. Frankness when the news is bad. Honesty when the chart turns. Integrity when the founder leaves. Zero extraction when it would be easy to hide one more line in the split.

The test, applied to five rooms

On the token. Is there a founder line in the fee split? Is the owner renounced? Is the burn balance checkable? Zero extraction means: no hidden allocation, no treasury privilege, no drain.

On the story. Does the fiction recruit a cult, or does it describe a world? Does it ask you to kneel, or ask you to look? Zero extraction means: no secret-order logic, no blood-lineage sacrament, no fifth god.

On the macro rail. Are the claims layered, or are they smuggled? Does the document state its falsifiers? Zero extraction means: no certainty sold as fact, no conspiracy sold as hope.

On the Archive. Are the masks masks, or are they promoted into operators? Zero extraction means: no racial ranking, no atrocity tourism, no secret recruitment.

On the LP. Is the pool branded, or is it a ticker? Is the doorway named, or is it a hopium graph? Zero extraction means: promote the address you own, don't pretend to own the chain.

Hierarchy of postulates · bottom → up

  1. Life first — existence that can continue and learn is prior to any model of it.
  2. Bond / affinity — things that remain near each other under stress are already in relation; gravity is the large-scale name of that relation.
  3. Iteration — what endures is what can be tried again; time is the measure of distance from a zero of pure potential.
  4. Form / language — pattern that can be taught and reused (Thoth layer).

  6. Force under boundary — energy that stays usable inside a shell (Ra-Shen layer).

Crypto sits on this stack the same way cosmology does: fixed supply, burn, lock, and public bond are practices of non-extraction. Demand is not marketing first; it is people who stay because the relation is real.
