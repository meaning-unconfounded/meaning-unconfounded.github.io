🥇 First · The Settlement

pDAI 💵

What settlement is for.

Every serious chain eventually needs one thing the rest of the graph can price against. On PulseChain, the candidate is pDAI.

pDAI is the Ethereum-genesis copy of DAI, running on PulseChain at its original address. It is not a new token. It is a fork-inherited contract with an existing market history on the other side of the bridge. That is what makes it credible as a candidate: it already has relationships.

What it is3pool base coin · FEI's conversion asset · input to a registered Synthetix DAI wrapper · output of remaining Maker savings-credit conversion
Proposed roleCommon settlement and pricing asset across connected markets

The thesis. A funded two-sided dollar policy. Buy below the target. Meet demand with target-price offers. Restore the inputs consumed. An independent executable dollar reference determines payment amounts. That is the whole rule.

The five jobs of every route

Every path the thesis describes, and there are nine named routes, performs the same five jobs. That is the load-bearing structure. That is what a route is.

  1. Source funding — where the capital comes from.
  2. Destination acceptance — whether the other side will accept the input.
  3. Funded conversion — the swap through an issuer, a Curve pool, a Balancer pool, or another market.
  4. Price-sensitive trading — the target-price side of the trade.
  5. Replenishment — the loop back to capital that is available again.

The thesis's own confidence numbers

The thesis does not sell certainty. It states its own confidence, its own numbers, printed on its own page.

H1 — a source receipt can finance a local purchase if accepted and funded: 99%, range 97–100%.

H2 — the complete route is executable at the pinned snapshot: 1%, range 0–3%.

H3 — validator/admin control and nominal copied collateral alone suffice for target: 0.5%, range 0–2%.

H4 — durable: 0.5%, range 0–2%.

The gates the thesis names as blocking its own route today: all 17 Aave Portal unbacked caps sit at zero; sETH2 token logic reads revert; ordinary Maker PSM is disabled with Vat live set to zero; the sampled Aave DAI reserve held zero pDAI cash at the saved checkpoint. These are the gates. The thesis names them itself.

Why pDAI matters is not that it will hit a dollar tomorrow. It matters that if the graph ever needs a settlement unit, the thesis describing how it would work is already written, with its own gates, its own confidence numbers, its own falsifiers. That is a serious object. That is why pDAI comes first.

Dysnomia is the virtual machine, the System0. A mathematical universe of smart contracts where actions change on-chain state through modular exponentiation. 🫀 Fourth · The Floor

PulseChain

Faster · Cheaper · Stronger

You already know you are on PulseChain. This section states what the floor is, for the record.

PulseChain is a proof-of-stake fork of Ethereum, created in 2023 by Richard Heart and the PulseChain community. Its native token is PLS. Its DEX is PulseX. Gas is cheap enough that serious projects iterate without burning working capital.

Two facts make it more than a venue.

The copy. PulseChain forked Ethereum's state. Every ERC-20 that existed at the snapshot got a PRC-20 mirror at the same address. This is why the pDAI thesis can name its contracts at their original Ethereum addresses, because PulseChain inherited them. The chain is a copy of an economic state, running on cheaper rails.

The community run. Much of the ecosystem runs on community infrastructure, IPFS-hosted interfaces, community nodes, public RPCs. The chain does not depend on a single corporate operator to stay live. What holds when the operator stops is what was already held by the community. Same law as everything else in this book.

The bridge · ERC-20 → PRC-20

What EROS is doing, and why it matters

The mission is simple. Bring TitanX-family and Ethereum-side ERC-20 exposure across the bridge to PulseChain, as PRC-20, so the community's coins have real bonded depth on the chain where the community actually runs.

The mechanism is already there. The bridge at bridge.pulsechain.com is live. What remains is the pattern: bridging, bonding, and pairing. That pattern is the actual work.

The pattern

  1. Bridge. Move ERC-20 exposure to PulseChain on the official bridge. One chain at a time. Verify each transaction.
  2. Bond. Pair the bridged asset with EROS, or with another community coin, on PulseX. Own the LP. Promote the pair.
  3. Deepen. Let the LP collect trading fees when the pair gets activity. Use the fees to deepen further, or add to the world, or hold as the pair grows.
  4. Repeat. Every pair deepens the wider web. Every pair makes the next pair cheaper to bridge, easier to bond, harder to ignore.

Why the mission matters

An island of depth is a farm. A web of depth is an economy. Every time a community coin becomes a PulseChain-native pair, every time an ERC-20 becomes a PRC-20 with bonded depth on the PulseChain side, the whole graph gets thicker. Arb bots link pools. Theme-branded LPs link people. Bonded edges link tribes.

This is the EROS role in the base's graph. Not to own the graph. To thicken it. Not to merge with the base. To bond to it.

The bridge is not only for coins. Every ERC-20 that crosses to PRC-20 is a step. Every builder who finds a home on the other side is a whole neighbourhood. The bridge is how the right people arrive, not as exit liquidity for someone else's story, but as co-authors of the world they will live in.

🌉 Fifth, continued · The Crossing

EROS 💕 Crossing the bridge 🌉

What a crossing actually is, what it is not, and how to do it without trusting a badge.

A bridge is only a road until something is built at the other end. The crossing is the first half. The bond is the second.

Two kinds of PRC-20, not one

This is the point that most readers miss, and the one that keeps a crossing honest. A token on PulseChain can reach you by two different routes, and the two are different contracts.

Fork-carried (the "p" assets)Copies of Ethereum's state at the fork, sitting at the same address as on Ethereum. pDAI, pUSDC, pUSDT, pWETH and pWBTC are examples. The Atropa Terminal describes them as "de-pegged but liquid."
Bridge-wrapped (the "from Ethereum" assets)Tokens the official bridge issues when you cross, at their own addresses and with names ending "from Ethereum." The Terminal lists eDAI, eUSDC, eUSDT and eWETH here and says they are distinct contracts.

The consequence. Bridging DAI from Ethereum does not give you pDAI. The Terminal states the two are different contracts on different origins, and that the "e" and "p" prefixes are informal. Any pairing or valuation that silently mixes them is wrong by construction. Before you bond, open the pair and read the token's contract address, not its ticker.

The crossing, in six moves

  1. Verify the instrument. On a bookmarked explorer, paste the EROS contract 0x0f1B7cfFC37547C5d189B08F255B60aa394C62cf, read owner() (renounced reads as the zero address), and compare totalSupply with the balance at the burn sink 0x…0369. The Ethereum twin 0xc0366e013BDD46946b7F22118E1af489b1693e0f exists with thinner liquidity.
  2. Open the official bridge from your bookmark. bridge.pulsechain.com only. Not a chat link, not a "support" message.
  3. Cross a small amount first. Confirm it arrives. Read the receipt on the explorer. Size up only after the small one lands.
  4. Read the arriving token's address. Match it to the pair you intend to join. Ticker is not proof.
  5. Bond on PulseX. Pair it with EROS or another community coin. Remember that EROS carries a 5% flow fee, so buys typically show about 6% slippage once price impact is included. Do a tiny test swap before size.
  6. Promote the pool address. The doorway you own is the one you can point to. Share the pair's address, not a graphic.

What the bridge cannot do

  • It cannot make a crossed token worth more. It moves exposure; the market sets the price.
  • It cannot protect you from a wrong contract, a fake site or a "support" DM. Phishing is the common loss.
  • It does not remove bridge risk. Bridge, smart-contract, thin-liquidity, concentration and impermanent-loss risks all stay with you, as the field manual's risk register sets out.
  • It does not merge anything. A bonded pair links two communities. It does not combine their supplies, their rules or their promises.
  • It does not bring the right people on its own. The crossing moves the coin. The invitation moves the builder.

Frankness. Nothing in this chapter is advice or a promise. Fees, timings and limits on the bridge belong to the bridge; read them there. Only risk what you can hold through silence as well as noise.

◉ Sixth · The Compounding Loop

Being found

Why price leads attention, and why preparation is the only edge.

The mechanics of the bridge are done. What remains is the harder question: how does any of this get found by people who were not already looking?

Crypto is an attention market before it is anything else. Capital follows attention. Attention follows movement. When the chart is quiet, the room is quiet. When the chart moves, strangers arrive, holders speak, and people who never bothered to investigate start asking questions.

This is not a moral judgment about how markets work. It is the weather.

The market psychology, stated plainly

Most people enter a market after momentum is already visible. They wait for the crowd, for the chart to confirm, for someone else to go first. That is not cowardice, it is how the human nervous system reads uncertainty. The crowd reduces risk, and the crowd only appears after price has already moved.

But the crowd is not where the edge lives. The edge lives one step earlier. The person who arrives before the confirmation, who reads the material while the room is still small, who studies the architecture while the chart is still quiet, that person buys the best ratio by construction. Not because they are braver. Because they did the reading.

The best price is not a reward for luck. It is a reward for preparation. The market does not pay for courage. It pays for having done the work before the work was obvious.

What the compounding loop actually is

When a project gets attention, that attention is not a one-time event. It is a rate. Each new participant, reader, holder, builder, increases the chance that the next arrival happens. Early readers become writers. Writers become welcomers. Welcomers become builders. Builders make the room slightly larger for the next arrival. Nothing in that chain depends on price going up forever. Everything in it depends on the room being worth returning to.

This is why the fourteen books matter more before the crowd than after it. A safe space teaching without a rug, with a positive agenda, no extraction, is most valuable to the people who find it early, while the room is still small enough that one person's contribution changes the shape of what is here.

Being early is not a race against other buyers.
It is a race against your own unpreparedness.

The two tracks, read, or act

Not everyone is ready to buy today. That is fine. There are two tracks into this project, and both are legitimate.

  • The reading track. Study the material. Read the Dossier, the Narrative, the Bridge, the Platinum Standard, the Hopeful Future. Understand what the stack actually is. Understand what the risk register actually says. Understand what you would be participating in if you did. Preparation is not a delay. It is the work that makes any future decision possible.
  • The action track. For the reader who has already done the work and decided, the position is available now. Not after the run. Not after the confirmation. Now. Bag, bond, commit, create. The material exists so that this decision can be made with clear eyes.

Both tracks converge on the same moment: the moment the chart moves and the crowd finally arrives. The reader who studied is ready to act on their own determination. The reader who acted early already has the position. Neither waits for the crowd.

What we cannot do, and what we can

We cannot command the price. Nobody can. Price is made by liquidity, demand, market conditions, holder behaviour, and risk perception, most of which lies outside any community's control.

What we can do is make sure that when the look finally comes, by whatever route, in whatever quarter, there is something real for it to find. Not a slogan. Not a chart with a chat. The full stack, the fourteen books, the receipts, the risk register, the honest confidence numbers, the safe room with no rug.

The gift is not a promise that price goes up. The gift is that when attention finally arrives, and it will, one way or another, what the arrival finds is worth staying for.
